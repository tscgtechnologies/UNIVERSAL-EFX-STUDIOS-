OUR CORE SERVICES

Professional VFX solutions built around precision and quality. Production-proven workflows for feature films, episodic television, commercials, and digital media.

CARD 01 • ROTOSCOPING

ROTOSCOPING

Accurate subject and object isolation for complex production shots. Our roto department produces sub-pixel accurate matte extractions with smooth organic edges and motion blur compensation.

Rotoscopy Matte Creation Hair & Edge Work Complex Shapes Motion Blur Mattes Silhouette FX

Key Deliverables: Multi-channel alpha mattes, RGBA plates, Nuke shape scripts, and Silhouette project packages ready for compositing integration.

CARD 02 • CLEANUP / PAINT

CLEANUP / PAINT

Clean and restore footage by removing unwanted elements and imperfections. We seamlessly remove stunt rigs, safety harnesses, overhead booms, and tracking markers while maintaining camera grain.

Object Removal Wire Removal Rig Removal Plate Cleanup Marker Cleanout Grain Matching

Key Deliverables: Clean plates with original 35mm film grain preservation, 2.5D projection cleanups, and patch mattes.

CARD 03 • MATCHMOVE

MATCHMOVE

Accurate tracking and camera solutions for seamless visual effects integration. We solve complex dynamic camera trajectories, focal length breathing, and object paths.

Camera Tracking Object Tracking 3D Tracking Scene Reconstruction Lens Distortion Solving 3DEqualizer

Key Deliverables: Alembic (.abc) camera solves, Maya scene files (.ma), 3D point cloud coordinates, and undistortion grids.

CARD 04 • COMPOSITING

COMPOSITING

Combine live action, CG and visual elements into seamless final shots. Combining photorealistic 3D renders with live action plates, matching depth of field, atmospheric illumination, and lens physics.

Keying CG Integration Matte Integration Color Matching Deep Compositing Foundry NukeX

Key Deliverables: Final master comp frames in 16-bit / 32-bit float OpenEXR, ProRes 4444 XQ, and client-specific conform packages.
