ABOUT UNIVERSAL EFX

BUILT FOR CREATIVE TEAMS

Universal EFX Studios Pvt Ltd is a VFX and post-production studio based in Secunderabad, Hyderabad. We specialize in rotoscopy, cleanup and paint, matchmove and compositing, helping creative teams turn production footage into polished final shots.

Whether handling high-volume rotoscoping, complex rig-paint outs, camera tracking under aggressive lens distortion, or multi-pass CG compositing, our pipeline provides filmmakers, episodic supervisors, and agencies with pixel-accurate deliverables on schedule.
